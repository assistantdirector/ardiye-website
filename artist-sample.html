Nazım Kahramantürk

Filmmaker / Director / Editor

Nazım Kahramantürk is a filmmaker, director, and film editor. As a co-founder of the 'ardiyé' collective art space, his practice focuses on the intersection of raw cinematography, post-production workflows, and structural decay.

Continuing his education in the Film Design and Direction department at Dokuz Eylül University Fine Arts Faculty, his recent works expand beyond traditional screen media into multi-layered audiovisual and mixed media installations, such as Protocol: NovAqua. His approach deliberately rejects polished aesthetics, instead documenting the technical and physical entropy of the creative process itself.
