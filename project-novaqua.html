Protocol: NovAqua

Mixed Media Installation / 2026
Medium Water, NovAqua, Rusted Steel
Live Feed APS-C Macro Observation
Artist Nazım Kahramantürk

Protocol: NovAqua repurposes a commercial water conditioner—typically used to neutralize heavy metals and provide a synthetic slime coat for aquatic life—as an agent of artificial preservation.

Over the course of the exhibition, NovAqua is introduced into industrial basins via a slow-drip mechanical system. Instead of sustaining organic life, the chemical binds to the heavy metals of the rusting steel, forming a visible, synthetic mucous layer over the decay. The chemical entropy is continuously monitored by a fixed macro lens on an APS-C sensor.
