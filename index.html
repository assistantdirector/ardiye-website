RAW FLUX.

Narrative

Ardiye is a space for the unfinished. We collect the raw, the unpolished, and the fragmented.

Acting as an archive for continuous creation, we step away from traditional curation. We provide a platform where the texture of the process is the focal point.

There is no pursuit of the perfect result here; only the documentation of the flux. Every visual glitch, unrefined cut, and raw noise is cataloged as a definitive piece of the work.

Residents

Nazım Kahramantürk

Producer

Nazım Kahramantürk is a filmmaker and editor. As a co-founder of the 'ardiyé' collective, his practice focuses on raw cinematography. Currently continuing his studies at Dokuz Eylül University's Fine Arts Faculty in Film Design and Direction, his recent works, such as Protocol: NovAqua, expand beyond the screen into mixed media, documenting the technical entropy of the creative process itself.

Necef Mengüverdi

Visual Artist

Necef Mengüverdi is a visual artist whose practice explores the intersection of spatial memory, raw materials, and fragmented narratives. Contributing to the 'ardiyé' initiative, her work challenges traditional curatorial boundaries through experimental media. She focuses on the latent textures within industrial environments, bringing forward the unseen layers of decay and transition.

Selection

[ MACRO FEED: CHEMICAL REACTION ]
Mixed Media Installation — 2026

Protocol: NovAqua

A continuous chemical reaction utilizing commercial water conditioner to create a synthetic slime coat over submerged industrial debris. A live study of artificial preservation and metallic decay, continuously monitored by a fixed macro lens on an APS-C sensor.

[ AUDIO WAVEFORM ]
A/V Installation — 2025

Ambient Architecture

A study of how sound shapes empty spaces. Layered ambient drones and slow guitar loops are mapped onto raw, uncolor-graded industrial footage. The project remains deliberately unmastered, forcing the viewer to confront the raw physical scale of abandoned structures.
