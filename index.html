There is no pursuit of the perfect result here; only the documentation of the flux.

Nazım Kahramantürk

Filmmaker / Director / Editor
[ PORTRAIT ]

Nazım Kahramantürk is a filmmaker, director, and film editor. As a co-founder of the 'ardiyé' collective art space, his practice focuses on raw cinematography, multi-camera setups, and advanced post-production workflows.

Currently continuing his studies at Dokuz Eylül University's Fine Arts Faculty in Film Design and Direction, his academic research is heavily anchored by his ongoing thesis, "MONTAJIN DEZENTEGRASYONU: DİJİTAL ÇAĞDA HIZ VE MEKANSAL ALGININ KAYBI". This theoretical foundation informs his contemporary installations, exploring how digital speed fractures spatial reality.

Utilizing robust workflows in DaVinci Resolve, his approach deliberately rejects polished aesthetics. Instead, his works, such as Protocol: NovAqua, expand beyond the screen into mixed media, documenting the technical entropy of the creative process itself.

Necef Mengüverdi

Visual Artist
[ PORTRAIT ]

Necef Mengüverdi is a visual artist whose practice explores the intersection of spatial memory, raw materials, and fragmented narratives. Contributing to the 'ardiyé' initiative, her work challenges traditional curatorial boundaries through experimental media.

She focuses on the latent textures within industrial environments, bringing forward the unseen layers of decay and transition. Her projects often remain in a deliberate state of flux, allowing the environment itself to continually shape the outcome of the work.

Protocol: NovAqua

Mixed Media Installation / 2026
[ WIDE WAREHOUSE SHOT ]
Medium Water, NovAqua, Rusted Steel
Live Feed APS-C Macro Observation
Artist Nazım Kahramantürk

Protocol: NovAqua repurposes a commercial water conditioner—typically used to neutralize heavy metals and provide a synthetic slime coat for aquatic life—as an agent of artificial preservation.

Over the course of the exhibition, NovAqua is introduced into industrial basins via a slow-drip mechanical system. Instead of sustaining organic life, the chemical binds to the heavy metals of the rusting steel, forming a visible, synthetic mucous layer over the decay. The chemical entropy is continuously monitored by a fixed macro lens on an APS-C sensor.

Ambient Architecture

A/V Installation / 2025
[ INDUSTRIAL WIDE SHOT ]
Cinematography Nazım Kahramantürk
Format Uncolor-graded / S-Log3
Sound Drone / Post-Rock Loops

Ambient Architecture is a study of how low-frequency sound and sustained visual observation shape our perception of empty, decaying spaces. Moving away from the hyper-speed of modern digital montage, the installation forces the viewer to confront the raw physical scale of abandoned structures.

The visual foundation relies on extended, static takes that bypass any polished color grading processes, preserving the native noise. Acoustically, the space is defined by heavy, slow guitar loops and thick ambient drones that mimic the spatial memory of the architecture.
