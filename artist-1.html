Nazım Kahramantürk

Filmmaker / Director / Editor
[ PORTRAIT ]

Nazım Kahramantürk is a filmmaker, director, and film editor. As a co-founder of the 'ardiyé' collective art space, his practice focuses on raw cinematography, multi-camera setups, and advanced post-production workflows.

Currently continuing his studies at Dokuz Eylül University's Fine Arts Faculty in Film Design and Direction, his academic research is heavily anchored by his ongoing thesis, "MONTAJIN DEZENTEGRASYONU: DİJİTAL ÇAĞDA HIZ VE MEKANSAL ALGININ KAYBI". This theoretical foundation informs his contemporary installations, exploring how digital speed fractures spatial reality.

Utilizing robust workflows in DaVinci Resolve, his approach deliberately rejects polished aesthetics. Instead, his works, such as Protocol: NovAqua, expand beyond the screen into mixed media, documenting the technical entropy of the creative process itself.
